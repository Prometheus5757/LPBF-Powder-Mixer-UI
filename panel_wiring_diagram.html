AUX-2 header (silkscreen order)

The header sits on the bottom edge of the board, with the power terminals on the left. Pins are at 2.54 mm pitch. D44 and A12 are free.

Input circuits

Wire the buttons active-high and read them with pinMode(pin, INPUT). On the breadboard, each 10 kΩ pull-down runs from the button's output strip to the bottom GND rail. A short jumper at column 1 bridges that rail to the top one.

I²C header → LCD backpack

WireRAMPS I²C (top → bottom)MegaBackpack

The RAMPS header runs SCL, SDA, GND, 5V from the top, and the backpack runs GND, VCC, SDA, SCL. The two orders are reversed, so the four wires have to cross once between the boards. The LCD takes 5 V from the Mega regulator through D1, which is fine on 12 V but not on 24 V.

Stepper drivers and motor headers

SocketSTEPDIRENABLEHeader B− B+ A+ A−Vref → Imax

All four sockets hold A4988s. E1 and the servo header are unused. The motor header silkscreen reads 2B 2A 1A 1B, so coil A (1A/1B) is on the right two pins and coil B (2A/2B) on the left two. With Vref = 0.45 V, Imax = 0.45 / (8 × 0.068 Ω) ≈ 0.83 A, about half the motor's 1.68 A rating. Plug the motors in before applying 12 V, and never hot-plug them.

What the render assumes

  • AUX-2 order: drawn from the RAMPS silkscreen. Top row: GND, A9, D40, D42, A11. Bottom row: 5V, A5, A10, D44, A12. The map in your wiring doc pairs some pins differently (A11 beside D42, A12 beside D44), so check the jumpers on the real header against this layout.
  • Pot order: Pot 1 (A5) is the knob nearest the RAMPS, and Pot 4 (A11) is the farthest. Confirm with the verification sketch.
  • Breadboard layout: drawn to keep every run straight. The buttons sit just right of AUX-2 and the pull-downs go to the bottom GND rail. Your real breadboard may differ, but the circuit is the same.
  • 12 V input: shown on the 5 A pair, which feeds the stepper drivers. Your docs don't say which input you used.
  • Motor lead colours: black A+, green A−, red B+, blue B−, the usual code for the 17HS15 series. Check your motor's datasheet.
