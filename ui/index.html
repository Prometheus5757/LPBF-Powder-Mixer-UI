    Calibration curves

    Grams per output revolution against speed, for each canister. Stored in this browser. Mass estimates appear once a canister has at least one trial.

    Clean dispensers

    Empties leftover powder by spinning the dispenser at full speed until you stop it.

    —
    1. 1Detach the powder canister from the dispenser.
    2. 2Put a catch tray or bag under the outlet.
    3. 3Pick the dispensers to empty and start. Stop once nothing more comes out, and run it again if you still see powder.
